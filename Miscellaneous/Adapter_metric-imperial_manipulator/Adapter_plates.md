# Adapter plates

[![Open GitHub folder]({{site.baseurl}}/assets/img/GitHub-Mark-32px.png) → to GitHub project folder](https://github.com/reiserlab/Fly-Lab-Gear/tree/main/Miscellaneous/Adapter_metric-imperial_manipulator){:.ifr}
These adapter plates were used to mount a metric micromanipulator to an imperically spaced breadboard.

![Adapter plates]({{site.baseurl}}/assets/img/Miscellaneous/Adapter_metric-imperial_manipulator/Adapter_metric-imperial_micro-manipulator_bottom.png){:.ifr .pop}
The top and bottom laser cutting designs were used to adapt a 3-axis micromanipulator LD40-LM with metrical mounting hole distances to a breadboard with a 1 inch grid. The top one was used to mount a pole in the center of the manipulator.

## LD40 Adapter
{:.clear}

`LD40_Adapter_Center.FCStd`: A FreeCAD-designed mounting plate that adapts the metric M3 screw pattern of the [LD40-LM](https://a.co/d/fMeQiEK) three-axis stage to an optomechanical breadboard with a 1" hole grid and 1/4"-20 tapped holes (as found in Thorlabs, Newport, and similar product lines). Two variants are provided as STEP files in the `production/` folder:

![LD40 Adapter Center]({{site.baseurl}}/assets/img/Miscellaneous/Adapter_metric-imperial_manipulator/LD40_Adapter_Center.png){:.ifr .pop}
`production/LD40_Adapter_Center.step`: A plate that mounts on top of the LD40 and provides a single 1/4"-20 hole at the center of the manipulator's top surface. This gives one standardized attachment point for accessories such as a pole or light holder. 


![LD40 Adapter 3in]({{site.baseurl}}/assets/img/Miscellaneous/Adapter_metric-imperial_manipulator/LD40_Adapter_3in.png){:.ifr .pop .clear}
`production/LD40_Adapter_3in.step`: An adapter plate that sits between the breadboard and the base of the LD40. It has three 1/4"-20 holes on the standard 1" grid: the center hole aligns the manipulator's center to the breadboard grid, and the two flanking holes fall outside the manipulator footprint and can be used to clamp the assembly firmly to the breadboard.
