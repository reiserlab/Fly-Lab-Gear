# Funnels

[![Open GitHub folder]({{site.baseurl}}/assets/img/GitHub-Mark-32px.png) → to GitHub project folder](https://github.com/reiserlab/Fly-Lab-Gear/tree/main/Tethering/Funnels){:.ifr}
Moving flies between containers without losing or injuring them requires matching the opening sizes at both ends. Fly rearing uses three container sizes that don't nest directly: bottles (~30 mm opening), standard 25 mm fly vials, and 12 mm test tubes used for cold anesthesia on ice before tethering. These funnels bridge the gaps between those sizes. Each funnel is a short tapered adapter that sits between the two containers; flies walk or fall through under gravity or gentle tapping.

![Funnel 1]({{site.baseurl}}/assets/img/Tethering/Funnels/Adapter_fly-vials-01.png){:.ifr .pop}
`Adapter_fly-vials-01.stl`: Vial to vial (25 mm to 25 mm). Used to consolidate flies from one rearing vial into another, for example when selecting a group for an experiment.
{:.clear}

![Funnel 2]({{site.baseurl}}/assets/img/Tethering/Funnels/Adapter_fly-vials-02.png){:.ifr .pop}
`Adapter_fly-vials-02.stl`: Rearing bottle to vial. Transfers flies from a larger stock bottle into a 25 mm vial for handling.
{:.clear}

![Funnel 3]({{site.baseurl}}/assets/img/Tethering/Funnels/Adapter_fly-vials-03.png){:.ifr .pop}
`Adapter_fly-vials-03.stl`: Vial to 12 mm test tube. The last step before cold anesthesia — moves a small group of flies from the 25 mm vial into the narrow tube that will be placed on ice.
{:.clear}

![Funnel 4]({{site.baseurl}}/assets/img/Tethering/Funnels/Funnel_fly-vial-to-12mm-tube.png){:.ifr .pop}
`Funnel_fly-vial-to-12mm-tube.FCStd`: Redesign of Funnel 3 in FreeCAD with an improved taper. This is the currently recommended version for the vial-to-12 mm-tube transfer.
{:.clear}
